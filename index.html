What is Khaana?

खाना, khānā, khaana

Khaana (खाना in Devanagari, khānā in formal transliteration) is a familiar Hindi and Urdu word tied to food, meals, and eating. Khaana.org explores how the word is spelled, what it actually means, where it comes from, and how the idea of "food" is expressed across South Asia's many languages.

Explore the word

How to spell it

khānā, khaana, or khana? Devanagari, formal transliteration, and everyday spelling, explained.

What it means

A noun ("food," "a meal") and a verb ("to eat"), with real Hindi sentences showing both.

Where it comes from

Its roots in Sanskrit, its shared life in Hindi and Urdu, and a common mix-up with an unrelated Persian word.
