About Khaana.org

What this site is, what it deliberately isn't, and how to flag something that needs fixing.

What Khaana.org is

Khaana.org is a small, independent educational site about one word: khaana, its spelling, its meaning, its history, and how the idea of "food" is expressed differently across South Asian languages. It's a companion to Khaana.com, which covers the food and recipes side of things; this site sticks to the word and the language behind it.

What Khaana.org isn't

A few things worth being upfront about:

  • It's not a nonprofit. ".org" is often read as a signal of nonprofit or charitable status, but the domain suffix itself carries no such legal meaning. Khaana.org is an independent site, not a registered nonprofit or charity, and doesn't claim to be either.
  • It's not a peer-reviewed linguistic source. The history and etymology page in particular is compiled from standard, widely cited reference material, not original academic research, and it has not yet been checked by a qualified Hindi-Urdu linguist. It's presented as a reasonable starting point, not a citation-ready authority.
  • It's not a duplicate of Khaana.com. Recipes, cuisine guides, and food culture content live at Khaana.com. This site links there rather than repeating that material.

Corrections and contributions

If you speak one of the languages discussed here, or have relevant linguistic expertise, and see something imprecise or missing, corrections are genuinely welcome. The same goes for regional words, expressions, or usages this site doesn't yet cover. Submissions and corrections are reviewed before anything is published or changed, rather than posted automatically.

To flag a correction or suggest an addition, reach out via email.
